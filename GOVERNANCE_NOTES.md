# Governance basis for the annual questions

Source reviewed: *The Gift of Governments: A Bishop’s Governing Handbook*, Dag Heward-Mills, 2nd edition (2022), from the user's connected Google Drive.

The renewal form uses ten areas drawn from the book:

1. Intention to continue or resign: willingness is treated as distinct from mere obedience (chapter 44).
2. Personal standing and readiness: accountable leadership, self-awareness, discipline and the need for help (chapter 24).
3. Oversight: care of churches, pastors, leaders and administrative systems (chapter 15).
4. Doctrine and church vision: bishops must understand the church’s faith, aims, terms and governing direction (chapters 3–5; benchmark in chapter 68).
5. Training and succession: continual training and appointment of pastors, overseers and bishops (chapters 9–15).
6. Councils: working through councils and implementing council decisions (chapters 31–38).
7. Communication, unity and isolation: open communication, church family, loyalty, practical connection and conflict prevention (chapters 39, 42, 44 and 89).
8. Administration and stewardship: reporting, finances, property and compliance with relevant laws (chapters 15, 30, 46–58 and benchmark in chapter 68).
9. Integrity and disclosure: blameless conduct, responsible use of authority and disclosure of matters needing help or review (chapters 69–75).
10. Confidentiality and recommitment: protecting sensitive information and renewing the vows and obligations of office (chapters 68 and 88).

The form is a declaration and review input. It does not score or automatically approve a bishop. Matters disclosed are routed to authorized church reviewers.
